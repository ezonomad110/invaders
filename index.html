SABER BREAKER

A LUMINOUS INVADER SAGA

The Empire's fleet is descending on the last free world. Wave after wave of fighters closes in — and one small Jedi stands in their way.

MOVE with the LEFT STICK. SWING your lightsaber with the RIGHT BUTTON.

Enemy bolts ricochet off the walls. Deflect them back! Every return builds that bolt's CHAIN — faster, stronger, and piercing one more enemy for every chain level.

Strike an enemy from the FRONT or the SIDE and the bolt pierces by chain, then returns to you. Strike one from BEHIND and the bolt REFLECTS — attack again and again from the rear, breaker-style.

Bosses hide inside the formation. Crack their cores. A droideka's shield must be stripped before its hull takes damage. And beware the Dark Lord himself: weak bolts he will swat back at you. Only a blazing chain — or a strike from behind — can touch him. Captains and capital ships fire bursts, fans, beams, missiles and splitters. Beams cannot be parried — dodge them. Missiles can only be smashed, and they come in volleys of three. The Death Star launches armoured brutes — crack them with chained bolts.

A bolt that slips past you is harmless. A bolt that hits your body costs one life. If the formation reaches the front line, all is lost.
